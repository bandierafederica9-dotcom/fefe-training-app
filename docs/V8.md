# Fede Training v8 — what was decided

Written down so it survives a lost conversation. Everything here is Federica's
decision unless marked *(mio)*, which means I proposed it and she agreed.

## The model, unchanged

One public app, no login. Her data lives only on the phone; export/import JSON
moves it between devices. No backend. A personal code is added only if the Claude
coach is ever built.

Hosting: Cloudflare Pages, `fefe-training-app.pages.dev`, production from `main`,
a preview per branch. Netlify is off.

## The plan

**The calisthenics is not a separate place.** She never opened the Lab. But when a
skill is the first exercise of a gym day she does it two times out of three — so
the skill stays inside the day, as exercise 1, with a card of its own: its own
background, the rung she is on, the next rung, seconds instead of kilos.

**The ladder.** `CALISTHENICS_LIBRARY` flattened in declaration order is a
progression per skill: `LAB_LADDER`. Five skills, 23 rungs. Her rung is stored per
skill under `fede:lab:step:<skill>` and **never inferred from history** — moving up
is an explicit choice.

**Giorno 3** — unchanged, Frog Stand already leads it.

**Giorno 4** — seven exercises become six:

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 | L-Sit *(skill, at her rung)* |
| 2 | KAS Glute Bridge |
| 3 | Cable Kickback |
| 4 | Hip Abduction Seated |
| 5 | Hip Adduction |
| 6 | Cable Crunch |

Out: **Back Extension Glute Bias** (skipped 2 clean sessions out of 3), **Dead Bug**
(2 of 3, and already a rung of the Core & Compression ladder), **Vacuum addominale**
(3 of 3 — never done). Abduction and Adduction are the same machine back to back.

**The Core day is removed from the schedule.** It held exactly three exercises and
was never trained once in fourteen recorded sessions. Core now lives inside the
days: Hanging Knee Raise in Giorno 3, Cable Crunch in Giorno 4.

**Giorno 5 variant B** stays available but hidden.

Her history is never touched by any of this.

## The interface

Taken from Queenz Fitness — the structure, not the look. Her palette and type stay.

- **Home**: greeting, *Piano di oggi*, a segmented progress bar, then cards — round
  coloured icon, title, the useful figure underneath (*6 esercizi · 45 min*), chevron.
  Under the cards, three figures with a micro-line each.
- **One exercise at a time**, with *Precedente / Successivo* at the foot. Never seven
  cards in a column. This is the answer to "scheda pesante".
- **Exercise screen**: numbered title, photo, collapsible instructions, three icons
  (history, notes, save), then one row per set with the labels above the fields —
  `1ª Ripetizioni [ ] Peso kg [ ] ✓` — and a large green circle, not a checkbox.
- **Progressi**: tabs, the real chart.

Not taken: the donut (a single ratio is a meter, not a ring), chat, videos, recipes,
online classes (their business model, not hers), and their pink.

## The fixes she asked for

- **D** — the field follows the exercise, not the prose. `misura: tempo|ripetizioni`
  and `carico: corpo libero|esterno` as explicit properties. No kilos on L-Sit,
  Frog Stand, Vacuum, Plank. Today `isTimedExercise` greps the word "sec" in the
  prescription string, so the same exercise gets different fields in different days.
- **F** — a *+ Aggiungi esercizio* button inside the session. Today the only way in
  is the coach's keyword parser over a fixed list of six.
- **E** — unilateral on one row: enter right, left prefills the same, touch it only
  when it differs, and the difference is recorded. Six entries become three. She has
  already written *"meno forza nel braccio dx che nel sx"* in a note; that belongs in
  the data.
- **Onboarding** — it marks itself complete without asking anything, which is why her
  profile is empty. A test must forbid `setupComplete = true` with empty fields.
- **Double sessions** — on 13 October the app showed nineteen exercises, from three
  different days. It stopped after 22 October, but a test must forbid a day whose
  exercises come from more than one day.

## The chart

Eleven sessions over six weeks is thin. Two of the five exercises with three or more
sessions are trending *down* (Face Pull 16.25 → 8 kg), which is almost certainly a
change of machine or grip, not a loss of strength: a chart must not present that as
decline.

- **Home**: three figures with a micro-line, not a chart.
- **Progressi**: weekly columns (the honest view with few points), and under it the
  per-exercise progression with one highlighted and the rest grey.
- One hue, darker = higher. The palette goes through the colour-blindness and
  contrast validator before it ships. No dual axis, no rainbow, no donut.
